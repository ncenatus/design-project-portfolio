Before finding the forces inside the individual truss members, I first needed to determine the external reaction forces at the supports. I drew a free-body diagram of the entire truss and used the equilibrium equations \(\Sigma F_x=0\), \(\Sigma F_y=0\), and \(\Sigma M=0\). Taking moments allowed me to solve for the vertical reactions, and because there were no external horizontal loads, the horizontal reaction was zero.

From my calculations, I obtained:

$$ A_x=0 $$ $$ B_y=8.33\text{ kN} $$ $$ A_y=-8.33\text{ kN} $$

The negative sign for \(A_y\) means that its actual direction is opposite to the direction I initially assumed on my free-body diagram.
